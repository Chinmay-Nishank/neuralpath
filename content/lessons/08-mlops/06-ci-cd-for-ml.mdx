## Intuition: Never Promote a Model on Aggregate Score Alone

Suppose candidate model `v2` raises overall F1 from `0.88` to `0.90`, so you ship it to 100% of traffic—only to discover `v2` dropped accuracy on mobile iOS users or Spanish queries from `0.85` to `0.41`!

Production ML CI/CD enforces two ironclad rules:
1. **Slice-Aware Eval Gates:** candidate models must meet minimum quality thresholds on both global metrics *and* critical subgroup slices before merging.
2. **Staged Traffic Exposure:** run **Shadow Mode** first (mirror live requests asynchronously to `v2`, ignore its response, verify latency/crashes), then shift **Canary** traffic `1% -> 10% -> 50% -> 100%` with automated rollback on error spikes.

  Shipping a canary to 0.1% traffic for 5 minutes rarely achieves statistical power to detect a 2% conversion drop. Calculate required canary sample count $n \approx \frac{16 \sigma^2}{\Delta^2}$ before declaring a rollout clean!

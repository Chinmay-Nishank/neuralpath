## Intuition: You Cannot Improve What You Only Eyeball

Swapping an embedding model, shrinking chunk sizes from 512 to 256, or tweaking three words in a system prompt can silently break 15% of edge cases while looking fine on two hand-picked prompts.

Production AI engineering breaks evaluation into explicit layers:
1. **Deterministic Assertions:** valid JSON schema, citation IDs present, no forbidden PII leaks, latency under budget.
2. **The RAG Triad:** evaluate **Context Precision/Recall** (did retrieval find the facts?), **Faithfulness** (is every claim grounded in retrieved context without hallucination?), and **Answer Relevance** separately.
3. **Calibrated LLM-as-a-Judge:** grade nuanced outputs against clear 1–5 rubrics and verify judge alignment (`Cohen's kappa`) against a human-labeled golden validation set!

<Viz type="roc-pr" />

<Callout type="warning" title="Watch Out for Judge Bias">
  LLM judges exhibit **position bias** (preferring Option A over Option B when paired side-by-side) and **verbosity bias** (rewarding longer answers). Always randomize pairwise order (`A/B` and `B/A`) and calibrate against human labels.
</Callout>

<Callout type="math-deep-dive" title="Evaluating Agentic Tool Calls Directly">
  Instead of grading only final prose, inspect tool spans directly for **Tool Selection Accuracy** and **Argument Correctness** against typed schemas—giving deterministic ground truth even on open-ended tasks.
</Callout>

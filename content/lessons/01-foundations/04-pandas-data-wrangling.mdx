## Intuition: Garbage In, Garbage Out

Before reaching for XGBoost or neural networks, experienced practitioners spend time looking closely at distributions, missing values, and impossible measurements. A single corrupted column or unit mismatch ruins downstream generalization.

<Callout type="tip" title="Never Impute Using Whole-Dataset Statistics">
  Computing `df["age"].fillna(df["age"].mean())` across the entire file leaks test-set distribution statistics into training. Compute fill values only on the training split.
</Callout>

<Callout type="math-deep-dive" title="Why Median Imputation Resists Skewed Outliers">
  For heavy-tailed features (income, latency, house prices), a single extreme outlier drags the arithmetic mean $\frac{1}{N}\sum x_i$ sharply upward, whereas the sample median (50th percentile) has a 50% breakdown point.
</Callout>

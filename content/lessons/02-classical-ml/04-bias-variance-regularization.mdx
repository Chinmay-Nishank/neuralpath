## Intuition: Too Rigid vs. Too Twitchy

An overly simple model (like a straight line on a sine wave) suffers from **high bias**—it misses systematic structure regardless of how much data you collect. An overly flexible model (a degree-12 polynomial on 14 noisy points) suffers from **high variance**—it contorts wildly to chase random noise in whichever sample you drew.

<GlossaryTerm term="Regularization">Regularization</GlossaryTerm> adds a gentle penalty for oversized weights so complex models stay smooth without losing expressivity.

<Callout type="math-deep-dive" title="L2 (Ridge) vs. L1 (Lasso) Objectives">
  - **Ridge (L2):** $L(w) + \lambda \|w\|_2^2$ shrinks correlated weights proportionally and keeps all features small.
  - **Lasso (L1):** $L(w) + \lambda \|w\|_1$ has sharp diamond corners on the constraint ball, driving irrelevant feature weights to **exact zero** (built-in feature selection).
  - **ElasticNet:** combines both L1 and L2 penalties.
</Callout>

## Intuition: Finding Structure Without Labels

When datasets have no target labels $y$, **unsupervised learning** uncovers natural structure:
- **k-Means** partitions points into $k$ compact clusters by alternating two clean steps: *assign each point to its nearest centroid*, then *recompute centroids as cluster means*.
- **<GlossaryTerm term="Principal Component Analysis">PCA</GlossaryTerm>** rotates coordinate axes to align with directions of **maximum data variance**, allowing us to compress 1,000 correlated features down to 2D/3D visualizations or compact denoised features.

<Callout type="math-deep-dive" title="PCA via SVD of Centered Data">
  Given centered matrix $\tilde{X} = X - \bar{X}$ with SVD $\tilde{X} = U \Sigma V^T$:
  - Principal directions are the right singular vectors (rows of $V^T$).
  - Top-$k$ projection is $Z_k = \tilde{X} V_k$.
  - Explained variance per component is $\sigma_i^2 / (N - 1)$.
</Callout>

rng = np.random.default_rng(42)

_, S, Vt = np.linalg.svd(X_centered, full_matrices=False)
var_ratio = (S ** 2) / np.sum(S ** 2)

# 2. 2-cluster k-Means loop
pts = np.vstack([rng.normal([-2, -2], 0.4, (20, 2)), rng.normal([2, 2], 0.4, (20, 2))])
centroids = np.array([[-1.0, 0.5], [1.0, -0.5]])
for _ in range(8):
    dists = np.linalg.norm(pts[:, None, :] - centroids[None, :, :], axis=-1)
    labels = np.argmin(dists, axis=1)
    centroids = np.array([pts[labels == c].mean(axis=0) for c in range(2)])
print("Converged centroids:\n", centroids.round(3))`}

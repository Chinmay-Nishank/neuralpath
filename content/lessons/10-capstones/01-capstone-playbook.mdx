## Intuition: Ship the Simplest End-to-End Pipeline First

The #1 mistake in ML projects is spending two weeks tuning a complex Transformer or deep CNN before verifying data splits, evaluation metrics, or end-to-end latency.

Follow the **NeuralPath 5-Step Capstone Loop**:
1. **Frame & Freeze Metric:** pick one primary metric matching business harm (`PR-AUC`, `F1`, `RMSE`, `Faithfulness`).
2. **Isolate Splits:** lock held-out test/eval splits *before* looking at features or prompts.
3. **Ship Dumb + Fast Baseline:** Logistic Regression, TF-IDF, or zero-shot prompt in under 30 minutes.
4. **Inspect Top-20 Errors:** read the worst predictions—most bugs live in labels, parsing, or leakage!
5. **Iterate Systematically:** change one hypothesis per tracked run and verify slice stability.
